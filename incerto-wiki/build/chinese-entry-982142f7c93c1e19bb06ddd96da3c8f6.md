# 中文入口 / Chinese Entry Point

The English pages are canonical for now.  Chinese pages will be added only
after the corresponding English pages belong to a stable release, so translation
does not chase every draft edit.

目前英文页面是权威版本。中文页面会在对应英文页面进入稳定版本之后再加入，避免翻译跟随草稿频繁漂移。

## Translation Policy

- Translation source: stable English release pages.
- Translation location: `content/zh-cn/`.
- Required metadata for future translated pages: source page, source version,
  translation status, and reviewer.
- Mathematical notation should remain aligned with
  [Notation](../notation/index.md) unless a Chinese page explicitly explains a
  local convention.

## Current Status

No translated concept pages are stable yet.  This entry point establishes the
route and rules for bilingual support without treating translation as a blocker
for the English concept model.
