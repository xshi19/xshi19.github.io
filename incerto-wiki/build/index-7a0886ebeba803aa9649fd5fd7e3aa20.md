# Glossary

Short, hover-on-link definitions for terms referenced throughout the wiki.
Each entry should be one paragraph or less.

---

## Black Swan

An event that sits outside the usual expectation model, has large consequences,
and is often made to look explainable after the fact. In this wiki the term is
used for exposure and epistemic limits, not as a label for every surprising
loss [@taleb2020scoft].

## Extremistan

A domain where aggregate outcomes can be dominated by a small number of extreme
observations, so tail behavior and scaling matter more than representative
averages [@taleb2020scoft].

## Mediocristan

A domain where individual observations are bounded enough that averages,
dispersion measures, and thin-tail approximations can remain useful summaries
for the aggregate [@taleb2020scoft].
