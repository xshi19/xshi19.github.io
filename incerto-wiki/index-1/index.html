Notation

This is the shared mathematical symbol table for Incerto Wiki. Concept pages, reading guides, proofs, and executable examples should reuse these meanings for symbols that appear across multiple pages. Page-specific symbols should be declared on the page that uses them, then promoted here only when they become common.

Conventions¶

  • Random variables are uppercase, such as XXX and YYY; realized values are lowercase, such as xxx and yyy.

  • Distribution functions are written FFF, survival functions Fˉ\bar FFˉ, and densities or mass functions fff when they exist.

  • Tail parameters are positive unless a page explicitly states a broader domain.

  • Asymptotic statements specify the limit, usually x→∞x \to \inftyx→∞ or n→∞n \to \inftyn→∞.
