Reading Guide: Statistical Consequences of Fat Tails

Taleb, N. N. (2025). Statistical Consequences of Fat Tails, third edition. arXiv:2001.10488v4

The chapter 1–10 guides use this edition, printed pp. 3–6, 7–21, 25–68, 69–89, 91–105 and 107–120; chapter 5 also covers appendix B, pp. 127–133, and chapter 6 covers appendix A, pp. 121–125. Chapter 7 covers pp. 137–152 and appendices C/D, pp. 183–188. Chapter 8 covers pp. 153–169, including its §8.6 derivations. Chapter 9 covers pp. 171–181 and Appendix E, pp. 189–191. Chapter 10 covers pp. 193–205 and Appendices F/G, pp. 207–216. Each guide maps checked section/page locators to the relevant concept notes.

Each chapter page is a reading guide that links to the relevant concept notes. It is not a substitute for reading the book; it is a companion that fills notation gaps, adds proofs, and connects ideas.
